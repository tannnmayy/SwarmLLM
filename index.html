AI Swarm
Team SE7EN

Your phone can't run a big AI model.
Your room can.

Phones, laptops, the PC in the corner — one code away from becoming a single machine. Each device holds a slice of the model; together they run the whole thing. Nothing to install, no account, and nothing you type ever leaves the room.

Works in any modern browser. Open it on a second device to see the point.

No install, no sign-up, no drivers. A locked-down work laptop can join in the time it takes to scan a code.

Share what you like

You choose how much of your device to lend. Take it back whenever you want — the room carries on without you.

Anyone in the room can ask a question, and the answer appears on every screen at once.

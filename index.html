WHAT IS AI SWARM

A group of devices,
running one model together.

A language model has to be held in memory to run. Most are bigger than any one phone or laptop has, which is why AI usually lives in a data centre.

AI Swarm cuts the model into contiguous slices and gives one slice to each device in the room. Every word takes a lap through all of them before it appears on every screen at once.

The part we actually built is the scheduler: it measures how fast each device computes and how far it sits from its neighbours, then solves the placement — who is in, in what order, holding which layers, and which one should be host. Other swarms deal layers by pledged memory in join order, so one slow phone sets the pace for everyone.

No install. No account. Nothing you type reaches a server.

WHAT IS AI SWARM

One model,
shared by the room.

A model needs more memory than one everyday device has. AI Swarm lets a room of them share the load — and, unlike dealing layers by whoever pledged most, it measures each device and solves the placement.

room CCPU
01

Open a link

A four-letter code, or scan the QR on someone's screen. No install, no sign-up, no drivers — a locked-down work laptop can join.

02

Chip in what you like

You choose how much of your device to lend. It gets measured, not trusted: real speed, real free memory, real latency to every other device.

03

Think together

Every word laps the room and appears on every screen at once. Take your device back whenever — the room re-plans and carries on.

Private by design. The signalling server introduces devices and then stops mattering: it has no message type that could carry a prompt.
